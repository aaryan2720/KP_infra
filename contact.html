Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a quote?

Typically, our engineering team reviews your project requirements and responds with a detailed, transparent preliminary quote within 2 to 3 business days.

Do you operate outside of Pune?

Yes, we serve industrial zones (MIDC) and commercial sites all across Maharashtra, including Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar, Nashik, and Satara.

What details should I provide in the form?

Please include your project location, type of work required (e.g. RCC, PEB shed, machine shifting), estimated dimensions, and any architectural drawings you have.

Can we visit your office or sites?

Absolutely. You are welcome to visit our headquarters in Warale, Pune. We can also arrange site visits to our ongoing or completed projects upon request.
