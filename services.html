What We Offer

Services Built on Expertise.

From reinforced concrete structures to heavy machine foundations — complete industrial infrastructure under one roof.

Service 01

Earth Work

We provide reliable Earthwork and Site Preparation Services for Industrial, Commercial, and Construction Projects. Our team handles Excavation, Soil Removal, Leveling, Backfilling, and Rock Breaking work using Modern Machinery and Skilled Operators. We ensure safe Execution, Timely Completion, and Proper Groundwork for all types of Development Projects.

Scope Includes
  • Digging and Excavation of Black Soil
  • Hard Rock Breaking and Removal
  • Soft Murum Excavation Work
  • Hard Murum Cutting and Excavation
  • Murum Backfilling and Compaction
  • Site Leveling and Land Preparation
Service 02

R.C.C. Work

We execute reinforced concrete construction for industrial buildings, factories, and commercial structures using high-quality cement, aggregates, and water ratios in strict compliance with IS codes. From column footings and raft slabs to complete superstructures, our teams deliver precision at every stage.

Scope Includes
  • Foundations, columns, beams & slabs
  • Brick plaster and structural finishing
  • Steel platform fabrication & erection
  • Industrial floor and shed base works
Service 03

Industrial PEB Shed Work

We design and construct pre-engineered buildings (PEB) and industrial sheds for manufacturing plants, fabrication facilities, distribution warehouses, private warehouses, showrooms, and data centres. Our experts use high-quality structural steel and cladding materials, ensuring durable and efficient large-span structures.

Scope Includes
  • PEB structure supply and erection
  • Manufacturing & fabrication facility sheds
  • Warehouses and distribution centres
  • Office, showroom & data centre buildings
Service 04

Heavy Machine Foundations

Industrial machinery demands precisely engineered foundations to withstand dynamic loads, vibration, and operational stresses. K.P. Infrastructure specialises in designing and constructing foundations for heavy machinery — including presses, CNC machines, compressors, and industrial equipment — ensuring long-term stability and compliance.

Scope Includes
  • Dynamic load analysis and foundation design
  • Isolated, raft and pile-supported foundations
  • Anchor bolt setting and grouting
  • Vibration isolation systems
Service 05

Infrastructure Civil Works

We undertake all types of road construction and repair, drainage networks, utility corridors, and site development works — for private MIDC clients and government contracts alike. Our teams are equipped for large-scale civil works across Pune district and the surrounding industrial belt.

Scope Includes
  • Industrial and approach road construction
  • Drainage and stormwater systems
  • Site grading and compound wall works
  • Government and ZP civil contracts

Construction Equipment Rental

Scope Includes
  • JCB, Tractor, Poclain DTH, AJAX & Dumper on rent
  • All types of DG sets on rent
  • Crane and lifting equipment supply
  • Operator and fuel support available
Service 07

Machine Shifting

We provide professional industrial machinery shifting services, relocating equipment safely and within stipulated timelines. Our expertise lies in transferring heavy machines without damage to any component, using innovative rigging approaches, specialised tools, and experienced supervisory staff.

Scope Includes
  • In-plant and inter-plant machine relocation
  • Rigging, skidding and lifting operations
  • CNC, press and compressor shifting
  • Re-installation and alignment support

  • Manpower deployment and maintenance scheduling
