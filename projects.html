Surveying

All Survey & Total Station Work

From transferring Design Coordinates onto the Actual Site to precise Field Measurements for Billing and Quantity Calculations — We handle the full Survey Cycle using Total Station Instruments and Industry-Standard Software.

Marking / Setting-Out Work
  • Establish Control Points & Benchmarks
  • Set up Total Station and take Backsight
  • Input Design Coordinates from Drawings / AutoCAD
  • Mark Column Locations, Grid Lines, Footings & Road Centrelines
  • Peg, nail or lime powder markings for visibility
Billing — Quantity Measurement
  • Field measurements — length, breadth & height
  • Levels for earthwork cut & fill
  • Before-and-After surveys for comparison
  • Road quantities — subgrade, WMM & asphalt layers
  • Concrete volumes — footings, columns & slabs
Data Processing for Billing
  • Download survey data to computer
  • AutoCAD, Civil 3D & Microsoft Excel
  • Area calculations & contour generation
  • Surface models & volume reports (cut / fill)
Billing Documentation
  • Prepare measurement sheets (MB)
  • Attach drawings and survey reports
  • Submit quantities for contractor / client billing
  • Cross-check with BOQ (Bill of Quantities)
Verification & Cross-Checking
  • Compare actual vs design quantities
  • Re-survey for disputed quantities
  • Verify distances and angles before submission
Skills & Tools
  • Total station instrument handling
  • Knowledge of drawings and coordinates
  • AutoCAD, Excel & Civil 3D proficiency
  • Surveying principles & the accuracy control
